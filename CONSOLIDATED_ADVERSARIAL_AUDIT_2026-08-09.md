# Consolidated adversarial audit — `gridiron_oracle`

**Scope:** `a58cacf` (Initial Public Release) → `HEAD` (`191e151`), 11 commits, 225 paths, +392,666 / −410. Local `main` is 2 commits ahead of `origin/main`.
**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Sources:** three independent adversarial reviews (referred to below as **R1**, **R2**, **R3**), merged and deduplicated. ~65 raw findings → **35 consolidated findings**. Where the reviews disagreed on a material point, the disagreement was adjudicated against source (see §4).

---

## 1. Consolidated verdict

**DO NOT SHIP for 2026 draft use. Do not present the headline eval numbers as evidence of causal model skill.**

All three reviews independently reached do-not-ship. They did so for overlapping but not identical reasons, and the merge is worse than any single report:

- **R1** ("do-not-ship live; ship-with-guards as retrospective research tool") judged the math real and the product unshippable.
- **R2** ("do-not-ship for draft use") agreed and additionally showed the draft rank is dominated by realized games played.
- **R3** ("DO NOT SHIP this tree") found a target-game feature leak that neither other review detected.

**The decisive merged finding is C-01.** `snap_pct_off` is populated from the target game's own postgame `offense_pct`, it sits in the default `FEATURE_COLS`, **and** it is used as the row filter that defines the training and evaluation cohort. I verified all three facts against source. This has a consequence none of the three reports states in full:

> R1 and R2 each independently recomputed the headline win counts on strict same-row cohorts and reported that the results held — and treated that as their strongest reassurance. R3 did the same. But all three recomputations drew rows from the *same* `snap_pct_off >= 0.34` filtered population. Their agreement is therefore **not independent corroboration of causal validity**; it is three reviews confirming the same arithmetic on the same contaminated cohort. The 20/20, 25/25, 5/5 and 24/25 counts are arithmetically robust and causally uninterpretable.

Downgrading R1's verdict accordingly: the tree is not safely usable as a *retrospective research tool* either, because the retrospective numbers are the thing under question.

**Merged claim audit result: of 18 stated claims, 0 PASS, 6 PARTIAL, 12 FAIL.** Individually the reviews recorded 3, 4 and 0 unqualified passes respectively; no claim passed all three reviews. Every claim that one review passed, another review or my own check qualified or failed.

**What is genuinely sound and worth preserving** (all three converged here — see §5): the causal *baseline* construction, walk-forward meta-fold structure, Ridge coefficient order-safety at the client layer, SHA-256 sidecar integrity, absence of 2026 contamination in artifacts, absence of secrets in public history, the gitignore repair, and the resolution of the 2022 collapse in the current artifacts.

## 5. Convergent non-findings — attacks all three reviews failed to land

These held under three independent hostile examinations and represent real engineering quality. **Caveat:** items marked ⚠ are undermined by C-01 even though the attack itself failed.

- **Ridge coefficient order-safety.** Reconstruction is by name, not position; a key-shuffled coef file returns the correct `(['lgbm','catboost'], [0.2,0.8])`. Order-independent by construction.
- **Position→global coefficient fallback.** A positioned lookup with a missing positional file returns `None`, not the global file. Genuinely fail-closed *at the client layer* (defeated one frame up — C-04).
- **No literal actuals fallback in the draft path.** No reachable code path sums `y_true`. `_fetch_model_ppr` is stack → DB → 404, and `adp_eval.evaluate()` logs an explicit refusal and sets `mode="unavailable"`. The failure (C-03) is that target-season OOF is itself not a draft-time source.

Numbers shift 0.05–0.15 MAE; no sign changes. **But all three drew from the same leaked cohort (C-01).**
- **Meta-fold temporal causality.** `_meta_walk_forward_cv` asserts `max(train_base_folds) < val_base_fold` per fold with real runtime asserts in all four learners, exercised by tests. Stack OOF correctly omits fold 0. No cross-season leakage in the fold structure.
- **No 2026 contamination.** `feature_matrix` max = 2025; every OOF ≤ 2025; zero 2026 projection rows; every CLI season parser caps. Verified three times.
- **SHA-256 sidecars are real, not ornamental.** Verified on load in the stacker; all 19 present sidecars validate with 0 mismatches.
- **No duplicate-row poisoning in shipped stacks.** Zero duplicate `(player_id, game_id)`. ## 7. End-to-end trace, merged

Three independent traces (Amon-Ra St. Brown WR 2024 wk2; Chris Godwin WR 2024 wk5; Adam Thielen WR 2025 wk1) agree on the mechanics and on where it breaks.

1. **Feature row** — `build_feature_row` copies the target row's `offense_pct` into `snap_pct_off`. Thielen 2025 wk1: `snap_pct_off=0.57`, same-game `game_logs.offense_pct=0.57`, `actual_fantasy_ppr=2.0`. **Weakest link #1 (C-01):** the row both contains and was selected by target-game participation.
2. **Base OOF** — LGBM and CatBoost per-fold predictions land in dated CSVs (Godwin: 14.299 / 16.595). Expanding season folds are time-ordered *across* seasons; the row features are not as-of-game safe.
3. **Stack** — inner join on `(player_id, game_id)`, meta-Ridge trained on strictly prior folds (causality assert real ✓). Godwin `y_pred=16.4452`; St. Brown `12.6696`; Thielen `5.6455`. **Weakest link #2 (C-06):** which stack file is read is decided by filesystem mtime — the only thing separating this trace from the collapsed `_20260807` file is a timestamp. **Weakest link #3 (C-21):** recomputing from the *shipped* coefs gives 16.3073 / 12.605 — the served object is not the evaluated object.
4. **Materialize → DB** — values byte-identical (`projection=16.4452`, `5.6454560408`), `max_train_season < season` ✓, run `stack_materialize_20260809T171811Z`. Floor/ceiling are a cell-wide constant ±MAD (C-11); `posterior_samples` NULL (C-09).
5. **Draft rank** — sums the player's target-season weekly `y_pred`. Thielen: 8 appearances → 42.70 → `model_rank=180` vs `adp_rank=142`, `value_vs_adp=−38`. **Weakest link #4 (C-03):** a correct weekly model becomes a leaky season ranking that knows how many games the player actually played.

## 8. Consolidated fix order

Sequenced by risk reduction and dependency — several later items are pointless before earlier ones land.

**Tier 0 — before any further evaluation is meaningful**

1. **Eliminate target-game leakage and rebuild from zero (C-01).** Define an `as_of` feature contract; remove or lag `snap_pct_off`, PBP, NGS and participation fields; replace the snap-based cohort filter with a pregame-knowable eligibility rule; rebuild features and retrain every base OOF and stack. **Until this is done, the magnitude of the model's edge is UNVERIFIED and should be assumed absent.** Every downstream number — win counts, gate evidence, A/B deltas, Spearman — must be regenerated after this, so do it first.
